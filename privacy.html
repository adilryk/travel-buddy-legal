Travel Buddy: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Travel Buddy is a student project: a travel app for Pakistan where people share trips, find places and plan journeys. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, username, email address, password (stored only in hashed form), and optionally your phone number, bio and profile photo.
  • Sign-in with Google or Facebook: if you choose it, we receive your name, email address and your Google or Facebook account ID. We do not receive your Google or Facebook password, and we never post anything on your behalf.
  • Content you create: posts, photos, videos, comments, likes, saved places, trip plans and community activity.
  • Location: only when you allow it, to show places near you and plan routes. You can turn location off in your phone's settings.
  • Chats with Humsafar (the travel assistant): the questions you ask and its answers, so it can reply and keep your conversation history.
  • Device information: a notification token, so we can send the notifications you have turned on.

How we use it

  • To create and run your account and let you sign in.
  • To show your posts, plans and profile to other users according to your privacy settings.
  • To recommend places, answer travel questions and plan routes.
  • To send notifications you have turned on.
  • To keep the app safe, for example by limiting repeated failed logins.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Services we use

  • Google Sign-In and Facebook Login, when you choose to sign in with them.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), to deliver notifications.
  • Cloudflare R2, to store the photos and videos you upload.
  • GIPHY, which receives the words you type when you search for a GIF.
  • Groq, which processes the text of questions asked to Humsafar to understand and answer them.
  • OpenStreetMap, OpenRouteService, Open-Meteo, Wikipedia and Wikivoyage, which receive only place names or coordinates (never your account details) to look up places, routes and weather.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and the content linked to it are deleted. See how to delete your data.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, change who can see your posts, turn off notifications and location, and delete your account at any time.
