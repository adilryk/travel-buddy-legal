Travel Buddy: How to delete your data

Last updated: 25 November 2026

You can delete your Travel Buddy account and the data linked to it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Profile → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account, profile, posts, comments, plans and saved items are deleted. If you signed in with Facebook or Google, the link to that account is removed too. To remove your conversations with Humsafar, open Humsafar → History and delete them before you delete your account.

By email

If you can't open the app, email ashgeminiryk@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Travel Buddy account". We will delete your account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Remove the app's access on Facebook

In Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find Travel buddy and remove it.
